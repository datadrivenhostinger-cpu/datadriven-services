## Why Email Marketing Still Delivers the Highest ROI

Email marketing consistently delivers an average return of **\$42 for every \$1 spent**, making it the highest ROI channel in digital marketing. But generic blasts no longer cut it—your audience expects relevant, timely, and personalised communication.

In this guide, we share the 10 strategies our team at DataDriven has refined across hundreds of campaigns.

## 1. Segment Your List Like a Pro

Sending the same message to everyone is the fastest way to hurt deliverability and kill engagement. Split your list by:

- **Industry** – B2B vs. B2C needs wildly different messaging
- **Stage in funnel** – Awareness, consideration, or decision
- **Engagement history** – Active openers vs. cold subscribers
- **Geography** – Time zones and local events matter

Segmented campaigns see **14.31% higher open rates** than non-segmented ones (Mailchimp data).

## 2. Write Subject Lines That Demand to Be Opened

Your subject line determines whether the rest of your effort matters. Use these proven formulas:

- **Curiosity gap:** "We analysed 10,000 cold emails. Here's what worked."
- **Benefit-led:** "Get 3x more leads from LinkedIn—without ads"
- **Personalisation:** "[First Name], your free audit is ready"
- **Urgency:** "Last 24 hours: Claim your free strategy call"

> Always A/B test two subject line variants before sending to your full list.

## 3. Nail Your Preview Text

The preview text (preheader) is the second line of copy your subscriber sees in their inbox. Don't waste it—use it to extend the subject line's promise, not repeat it.

**Bad:** "View this email in your browser."
**Good:** "Here's the exact sequence that booked 47 demos in 30 days."

## 4. Personalise Beyond First Names

True personalisation goes beyond `{{first_name}}`. Use behavioural data to tailor content:

- **Last page they visited** on your website
- **Products or services** they've shown interest in
- **Previous emails** they clicked vs. ignored
- **Company size or revenue** for B2B audiences

## 5. Optimise Send Times with Data

Stop guessing when to send. Run split tests across different send windows:

- **B2B:** Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11 AM and 2–4 PM local time
- **B2C:** Weekend mornings often see higher engagement for lifestyle brands
- **Re-engagement:** Send when subscribers are most likely to be in "discovery" mode

Use your ESP's send-time optimisation feature if available.

## 6. Use Plain-Text Emails for Cold Outreach

For cold prospecting, heavily designed HTML emails scream "marketing blast." A plain-text email that reads like it came from a person converts far better.

Keep it:
- Under 150 words
- One clear call-to-action
- Personalised to the recipient's company or role

## 7. Build an Automated Welcome Sequence

The moment someone subscribes is when their interest is highest. Set up a 5-email welcome sequence:

1. **Email 1 (Instant):** Deliver your lead magnet + set expectations
2. **Email 2 (Day 2):** Share your best content piece
3. **Email 3 (Day 4):** Social proof—case study or testimonial
4. **Email 4 (Day 7):** Address a common objection
5. **Email 5 (Day 10):** Soft CTA – book a call, start a trial, etc.

## 8. Re-engage Inactive Subscribers Before Purging

Before removing cold subscribers, run a **3-email re-engagement campaign**:

1. "We miss you" – remind them of the value you provide
2. "Is this still useful?" – ask directly if they want to stay
3. "Last chance" – tell them they'll be removed and give one final opt-in

This cleans your list, improves deliverability, and sometimes revives valuable leads.

## 9. Track the Right Metrics

Vanity metrics (open rates alone) don't pay bills. Track:

- **Click-to-open rate (CTOR)** – tells you how compelling your email body is
- **Conversion rate** – how many recipients took the desired action
- **Revenue per email** – the ultimate ROI metric
- **List growth rate** – new subscribers minus unsubscribes
- **Deliverability** – inbox placement rate, not just sent count

## 10. Keep Your List Clean

A clean list = better deliverability = more revenue. Run hygiene checks monthly:

- Remove hard bounces immediately
- Suppress unsubscribes across all tools
- Validate new signups with double opt-in or email verification APIs
- Warm up new sending domains gradually
